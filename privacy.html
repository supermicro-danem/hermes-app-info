Hermes: Privacy policy

Hermes is used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. No other person's account can use it.

Data read from Google services (mail, calendar) is processed on the owner's own computer to sort mail and prepare drafts. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own use, and not used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account third-party access page.
